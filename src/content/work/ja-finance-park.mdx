## Key decisions

### A backlog, not a shopping list

The RFP arrived as a list of features to build. I reframed it into a prioritized
backlog split across MVP, alpha and beta — so the question stopped being "which of
these do we build" and became "what is the smallest thing that teaches us the
most". The stalled team started shipping.
