## Case studies on the old site

### ITV Studios — "Studios Portal"
- **Role:** product designer leading an innovation initiative.
- **Problem:** production teams had no streamlined way to manage and deliver
  shows; workflow was chaotic and cognitively heavy across planning, execution
  and post-transmission.
- **Built:** a cloud platform acting as a centralised task hub with an
  AI-assisted UI, consolidating production tasks into a dashboard.
- **Scope shift:** started as a product for digital tasks, became service
  design affecting upstream and cross-departmental processes.
- **Stated numbers:** 25,000+ tasks completed monthly · £400k opportunity hours
  saved · 24 interdepartmental processes streamlined · 20+ distinct
  production-level task types supported.
- **Reach:** upstream process change let ITV’s global production partners —
  who had been working to differing standards and criteria — all deliver on
  the one platform.
- **Research quote (verbatim, from the old site):** “I’ve got 9,000 things
  that have to get done and the one I forget will end up being the critical
  one.” Attribution unstated on the source page.
- **Disciplines as originally listed:** research, strategy, service design,
  facilitation, mentorship.
- Almost certainly the source of the 2022 Broadcast Tech Awards nomination.

### The Home Depot — enterprise retail support
- **Role:** full-stack UX designer (engaged as contractor/apprentice).
- **Three problems:** truck-rental process needed refinement and its
  infrastructure assessing for franchise partnerships; store associates needed
  faster access to internal tools; paint-department managers lacked tools to
  manage departmental growth.
- **Done:** redesigned the truck rental system and infrastructure; refined and
  validated a single sign-on portal (autocomplete, multi-user switching, error
  handling); worked with the reporting team on localised departmental
  performance insight.
- **Methods:** guerrilla testing, rapid prototyping, validation, service
  blueprinting, stakeholder alignment facilitation.
- **Stated numbers:** 7 min 25 sec of login time saved per session · $5–10M USD
  forecast business-plan impact · 5+ products affected · scope of 2,000+ stores
  at ~150 associates each · paint department context of $2M USD/day revenue.

### TaxSlayer
- **Role:** contracting design partner. UX, UI, research, IA, native app.
- **Problem:** a self-service financial platform needing both automation and
  user control/confidence.
- **Done:** optimised native mobile login; onboarding guidance; streamlined
  navigation; COVID relief payment portals. Acted as design advocate inside the
  client team, challenging inherited dark-UX patterns.
- **Methods:** heuristic analysis, regular user interviews, rapid prototyping.
- **Stated numbers:** 50+ redundant pages removed · 4 new financial education
  and assistance schemes added · 65+ discovery and framing cadences run.

### 100 Shapes — design leadership
- **Role:** Head of Design.
- **Problem:** the agency had no personal development structure for its
  creatives.
- **Done:** role hierarchy and skills matrices; individual OKRs aimed at
  T-shaped growth and team-wide collaboration; pairing structures; learning
  and facilitation rituals; core design principles around experimentation and
  collaboration; meeting cadence set to support rather than tax the culture;
  seniority naming with visible progression.
- **Team:** several designers of varying seniority and specialism (number not
  stated).
- **Stated outcomes:** shared team language around principles, clearer
  progression paths, individual growth aligned to company objectives.
- **No metrics stated.** This is the one case study with nothing quantified.
